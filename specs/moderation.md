# Moderation

## Purpose

IME is a community, and anything people can create or publish on it can be misused: hate speech in a comment, a stolen album, a design that hides malicious code. This domain covers how the community reports problems, how the platform's admins review them, and what happens to content that is taken down.

## Roles

- **Signed-in users** report content.
- **Rights holders** send copyright claims, with or without an IME account.
- **Platform admins** review reports and take content down. At launch this is IME's creator; the tools are built for a small admin team.

## Reporting

- A signed-in user can report a release, a product, a comment, a design, an artist, or a user profile.
- A report gives a reason (hate or harassment, copyright, illegal content, harmful design code, spam, or other) and an optional note.
- A user can report a given item once.
- Reporting is available on web and mobile, wherever the item appears.

## Copyright claims

- A rights holder can send a copyright claim to a published contact address without needing an account. It is reviewed like any other report.
- When a release is taken down for copyright, the artist is told why and can contest the claim. If the claim is withdrawn or the contest is upheld, the release is restored.
- Artists whose content is repeatedly taken down for copyright are suspended.

## Admin review

- Admins see open reports, grouped by the item reported, oldest first.
- For each, an admin either **dismisses** the report or **takes the item down**.
- Every admin action is recorded with who took it, when, and why.
- Admin tools are **web-only**.

## What a takedown does

- **Release**: removed from the catalog and from every library at once, and the artist's managers are emailed. Once the takedown is final (it was not contested, or the contest failed), buyers are emailed, purchases are refunded from the artist's payment account, and the release's files are deleted. Until then the files are kept, hidden from everyone, so the release can be restored.
- **Product**: pulled from the store. Orders for it that have not shipped are refunded from the artist's payment account.
- **Comment**: deleted.
- **Design**: removed from the marketplace and from every release using it. Those releases fall back to the default layout, their managers are emailed, and anyone who paid for the design is refunded from the designer's payment account.
- **Artist**: suspended. The profile is hidden, everything is pulled from sale and from the catalog, and uploads are blocked. Listeners keep releases they own unless a release is taken down on its own.
- **User profile**: an offensive username or display name is cleared, and the user must choose a new one.
- **User account**: an admin can suspend a user. A suspended user can still sign in and use their library, but cannot comment, report, publish, or sell.

## Rules & constraints

- Only admins can take content down. Artists and designers withdraw their own work by archiving it (see [Releases & Music](./releases.md) and [Creative Pages](./creative-pages.md)); managers delete comments on their own releases (see [Community](./community.md)).
- A takedown is the one case where listeners lose a release they own. Withdrawing from sale, archiving, or a seller leaving the platform never does (see [Library & Ownership](./library.md)).
- Takedown emails tell the recipient what was removed and why.

## Out of scope / future

- Automated content scanning, such as audio fingerprinting.
- Telling reporters the outcome of their report.
- Appeals beyond contesting a copyright claim.
- A public transparency report of takedowns.
